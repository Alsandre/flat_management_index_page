Cozy Garden Apartment

123 Elm Street, Garden District

Welcome to our absolutely favorite property! This charming ground-floor apartment is where we'd stay ourselves when visiting the city. The French doors open directly onto our lovingly maintained garden, where Maria grows herbs, tomatoes, and the most beautiful roses you've ever seen.

We've furnished it with pieces collected over the years - the antique writing desk was Carlos's grandmother's, and the cozy reading chair by the window is where I love to sit with my morning coffee. You'll find fresh herbs from our garden in the kitchen, and please feel free to pick some for your cooking!

The apartment sleeps 2 comfortably with a queen bed that has the fluffiest pillows (we're a bit obsessed with good bedding). The kitchenette has everything you need to prepare simple meals, and we always leave a bottle of local wine and some homemade cookies to welcome you.
